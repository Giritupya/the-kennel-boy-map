JOHN RAINBIRD

I am an adventurer writer.

I set off on a quest to discover a story and write about what happens along the way.

Usually, it begins with a person and their relationship with something. It might be another person, a place, an object, something they've lost, an animal—or twelve—or simply their idea of who they are and where they belong.

I don't need to know where the journey ends before I begin. In fact, I'd rather not. Part of the adventure is discovering what lies beyond the next hill alongside the characters I'm writing about.

I used to think I created worlds. These days, I'm not so sure.

It feels much more like I'm discovering them.
