TALES FROM THE CAMPFIRE

The Frozen North

In the Frozen North, survival depends on following the rules.

Ninjhe has spent his life beneath the ice, in a world shaped by bitter winds, hard ice and the songs his people use to survive.

His parents are gone, and he believes Unlurth is responsible. His first Longhorn hunt will finally give him the one thing he needs: a suit capable of protecting him in the Above.

Then he intends to find his god. And confront him.
